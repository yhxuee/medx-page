Use of Med-X

Use Med-X for health records you own or are authorized by family members to organize. Your use of Med-X is also governed by Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). These terms add to it and do not replace it, and mandatory consumer rights remain in effect.

Not medical advice

Med-X is a personal health-record organizer and visit-preparation tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, drug dosing, or clinical clearance. In a medical emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.

Checking your records

Automated reading of printed reports, unit conversions, trend summaries, vaccine schedule reminders, and optional Apple Intelligence summaries are provided to help you organize information and may occasionally contain errors or omissions. Always compare recognized values against your original reports and consult a qualified clinician before making health decisions.

Your device and backups

Because Med-X stores your records on your device rather than on our servers, we cannot recover lost data if your device is lost, erased, or reset. Keep your original medical documents and maintain regular personal backups of your device.

Exported and shared information

When you export a doctor summary, PDF, spreadsheet, or diagnostic log, or share content with another person or app, you are responsible for verifying the content and protecting the exported file. Deleting a record inside Med-X does not remove copies you have already exported or shared elsewhere.

Availability and rights

Features may vary by device, operating system version, region, language, and granted permissions. Apple Intelligence is available only on supported devices, regions, and languages. Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.

Updates and support

Revised terms are published here with an updated version number and effective period, with prior versions available in Version history. For support or feedback, contact contact@yhxue.com and share only the information needed to help with your question.
