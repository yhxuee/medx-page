2. Emergencies & Symptom Screening Boundaries

During visit preparation, Med-X applies conservative pattern matching to highlight potential emergency red-flag symptoms and display local emergency telephone numbers. This check is strictly informational and is not a clinical triage clearance mechanism. An absence of alerts never guarantees safety. If you believe you or someone else is experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services immediately.

3. OCR Extraction & User Verification Responsibility

Optical Character Recognition (OCR) on photographed or scanned documents can misread digits, decimal points, units, or table rows due to lighting, creases, handwriting, or layout complexity. Med-X attaches source-page crops to extracted items so you can inspect and verify every reading against your original paper or PDF report. You are solely responsible for verifying the accuracy of saved records before relying on or sharing them with a clinician.

4. Local Storage, Backups & Device Loss

Because Med-X operates strictly offline without a developer cloud account, the developer cannot recover lost data if you erase the app, forget your device passcode, lose your iPhone without an encrypted local/iCloud device backup, or execute Reset all data. You are responsible for safeguarding your physical device and backups.
