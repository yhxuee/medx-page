REPORTS & CHECK-UPS

Paper reports in. Clear records out.

Import with the camera, your photo library, or PDF files. Multi-section annual check-ups are grouped neatly by department, paper ECG strips stay zoomable as images, and anything uncertain is highlighted so you can check it against the original page before saving.

TRENDS & FOLLOW-UPS

See how results change over time.

Results from different clinics are converted into your preferred units automatically, with each report’s printed reference range shown right behind the line. Follow-up intervals noted in reports can be added to your Calendar in one tap.

PREPARE FOR A VISIT

Walk in with a one-page doctor summary.

Organize symptoms step by step, jot down questions you want to ask, and generate a clean “For the doctor” page — safety alerts, active medicines, recent changes, and report conclusions ready to show full screen or share as a PDF.

FAMILY, MEDICINES & VACCINES

One calm place for the whole family.

Keep separate, private spaces for yourself, parents, or children. Track active medicines with duplicate-ingredient reminders, keep vaccination records up to date, and keep allergies and safety details easy to find when it matters.
