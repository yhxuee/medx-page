On-Device Recognition

Scan Paper Reports with Line-Level Evidence

Import via camera scan, private photo picker, or PDF/image files (up to 30 pages, 15 MB). Skew-corrected Vision OCR and layout XY-cut segmentation separate tables, captions, and columns across 7 diagnostic families.

  • Supports Lab, Pathology, Imaging, Nuclear Medicine, Endoscopy, Electrophysiology (incl. Paper ECG tracings), and Functional Tests
  • Automatically splits multi-section physical check-up reports into one linked bundle
  • Confidence badges highlight items to verify, with tap-to-zoom crops of the original line
  • Every edit keeps the original printed reading and full version history
Trends & Follow-Ups

Compare Across Hospitals & Years

Labs print different units. Med-X normalizes results using explicit catalog conversions into the unit your labs use most, drawing each report's printed reference band right behind the curve.

  • Automatic follow-up extraction (e.g., “repeat CT in 6 months”) with one-tap Calendar add
  • Condition history with numbered citations back to each source report
  • UTF-8 BOM CSV export compatible with Excel and Numbers
Visit Preparation

Walk Into Every Consultation Prepared

Organize symptoms with structured OLDCARTS intake, emergency red-flag screening, and a one-page “For the doctor” referral snapshot with full-screen handover mode and paginated A4/Letter PDF export.

  • Lists alerts, active medicines, out-of-range labs with prior values, verbatim conclusions, and your questions
  • Optional on-device Apple Intelligence history summary verified sentence-by-sentence against saved facts
Family Vaults, Vaccines & Apple Health

Separate Encrypted Vaults for Everyone You Care For

Each family member has an isolated SQLCipher database and device-only Keychain key. Track vaccinations, medicines, and read-only Apple Health data without mixing records.

  • Offline RxNorm medicine lookup (3,375 ingredients, 5,728 brands) with duplicate-ingredient alerts
  • 90-day read-only Apple Health summary, Watch Lead I ECG viewer, sleep stages, and iOS 26 Health Medications
