Privacy Policy

Med-X is engineered on a single premise: your medical records belong strictly on your device, encrypted under your control.

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Bundle ID: com.yhxue.medx.app

1. Zero Data Collection Summary

App Store Privacy Label: Data Not Collected. Med-X contains no networking client, no cloud backend, no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising tracker, and no remote model downloader.

In accordance with Apple's privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), NSPrivacyTracking is false and NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes is empty. Nothing you scan, type, import, or view in Med-X is transmitted to the developer or any third party.

2. Local Encryption & Key Management

  • Per-Profile SQLCipher Vaults: Each family profile is stored in an isolated encrypted SQLCipher 4.19.0 database. No shared global clinical table exists across profiles.
  • Authenticated File & Registry Protection: The profile registry is sealed with AES-GCM encryption, and imported source pages/attachments are stored with iOS Complete File Protection (NSFileProtectionComplete).
  • Device-Only Keychain & Face ID: Encryption keys are stored in the iOS Keychain bound to your device (never synced to iCloud Keychain). When Require Face ID is turned on in Settings, keys require biometric or device passcode authentication and sessions lock automatically on backgrounding.

4. System Services, Exports & User Sharing

  • Optional On-Device Apple Intelligence: On eligible iOS 26+ devices, you may enable Apple's on-device Foundation Models to rephrase your history summary. Processing runs locally; Med-X never requests a cloud fallback.
  • User-Initiated Exports: When you tap Export results (CSV), Share or print as PDF, or Add to Calendar, the file or event is generated locally on your iPhone and handed to the iOS system share sheet or Calendar editor only at your explicit action.
  • Doctor Mode Clipboard: Copying a handoff summary uses a local-only clipboard item set to expire after 60 seconds without Universal Clipboard handoff.
  • OS-Level Services: Apple Health synchronization, iOS device backups, and system speech/model asset provisioning are governed by Apple's iOS settings and privacy policies outside Med-X's control.

5. Data Retention & Permanent Erasure

You retain complete control over every record:

  • Individual reports and notes moved to the Recycle bin can be restored or emptied permanently.
  • Settings → Reset all data (confirmed by typing ERASE plus biometric/passcode check) permanently deletes every profile vault, encryption key, recycle-bin entry, learned layout, and cached attachment.
  • Uninstalling Med-X from your iPhone removes the local app container and all stored files.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Med-X's security design, please open an issue or contact the developer via the Med-X support repository at github.com/yhxuee/medx-page.
