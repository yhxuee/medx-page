Med-X Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026. Med-X is operated by Yuhang XUE. Contact contact@yhxue.com with privacy questions or requests.

Your records

Medical reports, lab results, trends, medicines, vaccinations, family profiles, visit notes, attachments, and settings are stored on your device. Reading paper reports, photos, and PDFs, and learning from corrections you confirm, happen on your device; your original documents are never uploaded.

Apple Intelligence

If you turn on Apple Intelligence in Settings on a supported device, Med-X uses Apple’s on-device model to help repair unclear report entries and draft a concise history summary. Processing stays on your device, and Med-X does not send your health records to our servers or any third party for it. You can turn Apple Intelligence off in Settings at any time.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, Med-X reads selected recent health metrics on your device (such as sleep, activity, heart measurements, watch ECGs, and medications) to display alongside your records for the device owner. Med-X does not write to Apple Health and does not transmit Apple Health data off your device.

Online pages

Core features in Med-X work offline and do not connect to our servers. When you choose to open our online User Guide, Terms of Use, or Privacy Policy in a browser, normal web connection information (such as your IP address and requested page) is received to deliver the page. These pages contain no advertising or behavioral tracking.

Feedback and diagnostics

Feedback and support emails send only what you write or attach through your mail app to contact@yhxue.com. If you turn on “Include diagnostic log (.mtxt)”, a desensitized log of the past seven days of app operations, timings, and error codes is attached. It contains no names, test values, diagnoses, medicines, notes, or recognized text. If you turn on “Include device information”, basic app version, system version, device model, and language details are included. Local diagnostics are never sent automatically, and you can inspect or clear them anytime in Settings › About › Feedback & Diagnostics.

Permissions and protection

Camera, Photos, Calendar, Location (used locally to suggest nearby emergency numbers), Microphone, Speech Recognition, and Face ID are used only for the features you choose, and can be managed in iOS Settings. Biometric verification is handled by Apple; Med-X never receives face or fingerprint data. Files you export or share (such as doctor summaries, PDFs, spreadsheets, or emails) leave Med-X and require separate protection.

Advertising and tracking

Med-X contains no advertising, includes no third-party analytics or behavioral tracking, and never sells or shares your health records. When you email support, we use the information you provide only to respond to your request.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain on your device until you delete them, empty the recycle bin, or use Reset all data in Settings. On-device learning and diagnostic logs can be cleared separately at any time. Files you exported or shared outside Med-X must be managed separately.

Your choices

You can edit, export, or delete your records, turn off optional features, and change permissions whenever you wish. Contact contact@yhxue.com with any privacy questions or requests regarding support messages you have sent us. Please avoid sending personal medical reports or sensitive health details by email unless necessary.

Updates

When this policy changes, we publish the updated version here with a new version number and effective period, and keep earlier versions accessible under Version history. You can open the current policy anytime from Settings › About › Privacy Policy.
